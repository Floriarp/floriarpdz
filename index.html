Questions

Frequently Asked

Common questions about our server rules and gameplay.

How do I appeal a ban?

You can appeal bans through our Discord ticket system. Create a ticket in #support and provide your Steam ID, reason for ban, and a sincere explanation. Appeals are typically reviewed within 24-48 hours.

What is considered "valid RP reason" for combat?

Valid RP reasons include: ongoing gang conflicts with prior interaction, self-defense when your life is threatened, defending property during active robbery, or police using appropriate force.

Can I stream on this server?

Yes! We encourage content creation. Add [FloriaRP] to your stream title. Be aware that stream sniping is against the rules.

How do I join the police or EMS?

Whitelisted jobs require an application on our Discord. You must have at least 20 hours playtime, clean record, and pass an interview.
